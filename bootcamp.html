This Year's Theme: Physical AI

PYNQ Bootcamp

Discover Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning with the AMD KRIA-SOM KV260 board.

This four-day summer course introduces students to the world of Physical AI — this year's theme — focusing on actual deployment and real-world utility of the ever increasing power of Machine Learning and AI. Physical AI means going beyond a model that just runs on a screen: a camera sees the real world, code decides what to do about it, and a real action follows, with no human in the loop. The course is a natural evolution of PYNQ Bootcamp offered in previous years. The camp will include a wide range of topics, such as using AMD technology to enable computer vision, as well as gathering real-world data using various sensors and feeding it into AI models. By the conclusion of the course, you will be familiar with AMD platforms and appreciate real world application of AI. This year's capstone is GridMind, a two-team robotics card-matching competition that is Physical AI in action: each team's own KRIA board sees, decides, and acts, built entirely on the skills taught during the week.

  • This camp is geared towards students with some basic experience with either coding and/or electronics. Some Python experience is required.
  • This camp runs July 27-30, hosted by the St. Vrain Innovation Center at Trail Ridge Middle School, culminating in a Competition Showcase on the final afternoon.
  • Additional learning experiences will be offered to any returning camp members
